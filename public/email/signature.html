Email signature

Fill in your details, then copy the signature and paste it into Gmail, Outlook or Apple Mail.

Preview — how it appears at the bottom of an email
Gmail
  1. Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new.
  2. Click into the box and paste (Ctrl/Cmd + V). Save changes at the bottom.
Outlook
  1. Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature (new Outlook / web), or File → Options → Mail → Signatures (classic).
  2. Paste into the editor and save.
Apple Mail
  1. Mail → Settings → Signatures → +. Untick "Always match my default message font".
  2. Paste into the signature area.
