## Caption

Some windows are meant to be looked at. Vista Guard is meant to be looked through. A thermally broken aluminium frame keeps the profile slim, so the glass does the work, and it still holds ±65 psf with large missile impact approval for Wind Zone 4, in and outside the HVHZ. No shutters needed. Picture windows to 59 x 98 3/8 in, tilt and turn to 48 x 78 in. Got an opening in mind? Tell us the size in the comments.
