Aluminium or PVC? How to choose your window frame.

A 60 second guide for anyone specifying windows.

01 / 05

It comes down to the frame.

Both hold glass. The difference is the material around it. Aluminium is strong and slim. PVC is warm and cost efficient. Everything else follows from that.

02 / 05

Choose aluminium when...

  • 01You want slim sightlines and more glass.
  • 02The opening is large or structural.
  • 03The look is modern and design led.
15/16 inSightline on the SlimPatio 68 aluminium slider.
03 / 05

Choose PVC when...

  • 01Thermal performance is the priority.
  • 02The budget needs to work harder.
  • 03Low maintenance matters most.
 
04 / 05

Still unsure? Start with the opening.

Large spans and slim frames point to aluminium. Energy and value point to PVC. Size and sightline decide it.
