Aluminium or PVC?

How to choose your window frame.

01 / 05
i. the difference
both hold glass

It comes down to the frame.

The difference is the material around the glass.

Aluminium
Strong · Slim
PVC
Warm · Cost efficient

Everything else follows from that.

02 / 05
ii. aluminium
when it wins

Choose aluminium when…

  1. iYou want slim sightlines and more glass.
  2. iiThe opening is large or structural.
  3. iiiThe look is modern and design led.

iii. pvc
when it wins

Choose PVC when…

  1. iThermal performance is the priority.
  2. iiThe budget needs to work harder.
  3. iiiLow maintenance matters most.
Warm, efficient and easy to live with.
