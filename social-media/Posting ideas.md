# VALDA posting ideas

This is a bank of post ideas, sorted by content pillar. When a week needs a post, pick one from here.

Ideas already on the Oct–Nov calendar are marked *(planned)*. Never invent a spec: every number has to come from a datasheet or from VALDA.

## 1. Opening styles (isometric drawings)

All of these use the same isometric drawing style as Tilt and turn (`social/tools/iso-tilt-turn.mjs`). Each one shows the window closed and open, with an arrow for the movement.

- Tilt and turn *(planned, 15 Oct)*
- Casement: inswing vs outswing *(planned, 20 Oct; add drawings)*
- Awning: hinged at the top, opens out. Good for rain and bathrooms.
- Hopper / tilt-only: hinged at the bottom, opens in.
- Lift and slide: you lift the panel to move it, and it seals down when closed. The VALDA film shows one.
- Slide and fold *(done, 8 Oct; could add drawings)*
- Pivot door
- Fixed vs operable *(planned, 6 Nov)*
- "Every way a window opens": all the opening styles on one carousel. Good to save and share.
- Why European windows open inward and most US windows open outward.
