# Tilt and Turn Windows in the US: What to Know

Walk down any street in Germany, Austria or Bulgaria and almost every window is a tilt and turn. In the US they are still unusual, and they are one of the first things people ask about when they see European windows. Here is how they work and what to check before you specify them.

## How a tilt and turn window works

One handle controls everything.

- **Handle down: closed.** Multi-point locking pulls the sash tight against the gaskets all the way round.
- **Handle sideways: turn.** The sash swings into the room like a door, hinged at the side.
- **Handle up: tilt.** The top of the sash leans into the room a few inches, hinged at the bottom.

## Why Europe uses them

- **Ventilation in any weather.** In the tilt position air comes in at the top, rain runs off the outside, and the window stays locked at the sides. You can leave it tilted when you go out.
- **Easy cleaning.** Because the sash opens fully into the room, you can clean the outside glass from inside, even on upper floors.
- **A tight seal.** Locking points around the whole sash press it evenly onto the gaskets, which helps with air leakage, water and sound.
- **One window, two jobs.** You get a ventilation window and a full opening in the same unit.

## What to check on a US project

**Screens.** Because the sash opens in, the insect screen goes on the outside of the frame. Plan for fixed or roller screens outside.

**Interior clearance.** In the turn position the sash swings into the room. Check that blinds, curtains, furniture and deep sills leave room for it.

**Egress.** Bedroom windows must provide an emergency escape opening under US codes. In the turn position, a tilt and turn opens almost the full sash, but confirm the clear opening size for each window against your local code.

**Impact rating.** On the hurricane coast, every window needs the right approval. MasterLine 8 by Reynaers is rated for large and small missile impact in Wind Zone 3, and ConceptSystem 77 is approved in and outside the HVHZ. Check the approval for your exact zone and size.

**Acoustics.** Tilt and turn windows seal well. MasterLine 8 reaches STC 50, which helps near roads and airports.

## Tilt and turn or casement?

A US casement usually opens out and is operated with a crank. A tilt and turn opens in and adds the tilt position for ventilation. Choose outswing casements where the room needs every inch of interior space. Choose tilt and turn where ventilation, security and easy cleaning matter most.

## FAQ

**Are tilt and turn windows allowed in the US?**

Yes. They must meet the same code requirements as any window, including energy, egress and, on the coast, impact ratings for your zone.

**Can tilt and turn windows have screens?**

Yes. Because they open inward, the screen is mounted on the outside of the frame.

**Are tilt and turn windows hurricane rated?**

They can be. MasterLine 8 by Reynaers is rated for large and small missile impact in Wind Zone 3. Always check the approval for your zone and window size.

**Can a tilt and turn window be used for egress?**

Often, yes, in the turn position. Check the clear opening size against your local code for each bedroom window.
