U-factor is the one number that tells you how much heat a window lets through. It covers the whole unit, frame and glass together, so it is the fairest way to compare two windows. Lower is better. Double glazing is a good starting point, triple glazing takes it further: on ConceptSystem 77 it moves the U-factor from 0.24 to 0.18, and MasterLine 10 reaches 0.14. When you compare quotes, ask for the whole-window figure, not the glass alone.
