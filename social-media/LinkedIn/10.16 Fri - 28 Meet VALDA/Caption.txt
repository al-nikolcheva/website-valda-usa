Meet VALDA: a family-owned European window and door manufacturer, since 1998.

It started with two founders, one machine and a small garage in Bulgaria. Today we make aluminum and PVC windows, doors, sliding systems and facades in our own two factories in Sofia and Veliko Tarnovo, with 300+ production specialists, our own glazing and our own coating line.

Still 100% family-owned. Still making every window as if our name is on it, because it is.

Since 2016 we have been bringing that work to the US, factory direct.
