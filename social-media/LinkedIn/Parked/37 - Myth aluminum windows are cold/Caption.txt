Myth: aluminum windows are cold.

It used to be true. Old aluminum frames were solid metal, and metal conducts heat straight through.

Modern frames have a thermal break: an insulating polyamide strip that splits the frame in two, so the heat stops there. On MasterLine 10 by Reynaers, the whole-window U-factor reaches 0.14 with triple glazing.
