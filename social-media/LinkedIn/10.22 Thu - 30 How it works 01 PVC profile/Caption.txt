How a PVC window profile is made, from powder to a welded frame.

1. The compound: PVC powder with stabilizers, impact modifiers and UV protection
2. Twin-screw extrusion through a die shaped like the profile
3. A multi-chamber profile that traps air to slow the heat
4. Galvanized steel reinforcement to carry the load
5. Fusion-welded corners, one solid frame
