Aluminum or PVC windows: which frame is right for your project?

It is the first question on almost every project, and the answer is simpler than it looks.

Choose aluminum for slim sightlines, large openings and a modern, design-led look.
Choose PVC (vinyl) when thermal performance, budget and low maintenance lead.

Size and sightline usually decide it. We make both in our own factories in Europe, so the advice starts from the opening, not from what is in stock.
