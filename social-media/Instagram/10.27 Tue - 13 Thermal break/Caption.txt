What is a thermal break, and why does every aluminum window need one?

Aluminum carries heat hundreds of times faster than the polyamide used to break it. So a good aluminum frame is really two frames: an outer shell for the weather and an inner shell for the room, joined by an insulating polyamide core.

The result: less heat lost, a warmer frame and far less condensation.

It is the first question to ask about any aluminum window. Did you know this one?
