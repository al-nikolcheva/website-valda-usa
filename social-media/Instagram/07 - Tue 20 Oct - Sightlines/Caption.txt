Sightlines are the visible frame around the glass. Slimmer sightlines mean more glass, more light and openings that read as a single pane. SlimPatio 68 by Reynaers runs a 15/16 in sightline on a sliding door. The trick is choosing the system early, before the opening sizes are fixed.
