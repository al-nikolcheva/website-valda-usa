STC, Sound Transmission Class, rates how well a window blocks sound. Higher is quieter, and a jump of around 10 points is heard as roughly half as loud. For traffic and aircraft noise, check OITC too, which weights the low frequencies. MasterLine 8 and MasterLine 10 by Reynaers both reach STC 50.
