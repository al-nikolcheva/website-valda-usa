Triple glazing: when it is actually worth it. On ConceptSystem 77 by Reynaers, the third pane takes the U-factor from 0.24 to 0.18. It pays back in cold climates, on noisy sites and on large glazed areas. It adds weight and cost, so make sure the frame is designed for it.
