Window U-factor, explained: the one number that tells you how much heat a window lets through.

Lower is better. But only compare the whole-window figure, frame and glass together. A center-of-glass number always looks better than the window on the wall.

On ConceptSystem 77 by Reynaers, triple glazing moves the U-factor from 0.24 to 0.18. MasterLine 10 reaches 0.14.

Quoting a European window? Divide the Uw value by 5.678 to get the US U-factor.
